# Endgame profession gold-making research

Research snapshot: November 27, 2026. These are candidates, not sales guarantees or ready-to-publish cards. We have no realm-specific price, turnover or demand observations. Check current recipe, vendor and game data before spending or publishing; test one customer-material commission or a small batch, valuing all inputs including self-gathered materials and applicable fees.

## Context and priorities

Blizzard announced [Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair opening December 9](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303009/world-of-warcraft-forever-raid-overviewlocations-raid-sizes-and-attunement) and recommends fire resistance for Onyxia. As of this snapshot they are future content. Do not label ingredients from unavailable raids as obtainable now; recheck availability when implementing.

Prioritize inexpensive, understandable crafts with distinct buyers and verified inputs. Prefer commissions where capital is high. Do not add auction-house epics to this slice. Candidate rankings below are editorial judgments, not observed profits.

## Tailoring

1. **Mooncloth — high-priority cooldown test.** Tailoring 230; Qia in Winterspring sells the pattern. [Forever spell](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18560/mooncloth) and [item](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=14342/mooncloth) show two Felcloth to one Mooncloth, at a moonwell, with a 3.958-day cooldown (about 95 hours). Potential buyers are crafters using Mooncloth; compare the value of both Felcloth and the limited cooldown to the sale/commission price. Verify vendor price and availability.
2. **Big Bag of Enchantment — promising specialist bag.** [Forever item record](https://wowforever.wclbox.com/api/open/v1/items/22249) says bind-on-equip, 32-slot **enchanting-material** bag, not a general bag; Classic's 24-slot figure is outdated. Research lead: Tailoring 255; pattern from Magister Kalendris, Dire Maul; 6 bolts of Runecloth, 4 Large Brilliant Shards, 4 Enchanted Leather, 4 Ironweb Spider Silk. [Recipe listing](https://wowforevertalents.com/professions/tailoring/specialty-bags/#r-27660) · [pattern lead](https://bindonforever.com/item/22309). Verify source and quantities against a second Forever source. Potential buyers are enchanters, but material cost makes a prearranged order safer.
3. **Felcloth Bag — secondary niche.** [Forever item record](https://wowforever.wclbox.com/api/open/v1/items/21341): 32-slot soul bag, Warlock only, bind-on-equip. [Recipe lead](https://wowforevertalents.com/professions/tailoring/specialty-bags/#r-26086) lists Tailoring 285, 12 Felcloth, 6 Enchanted Leather, 2 Dark Runes, 4 Ironweb Spider Silk and a spinning wheel. Pattern source remains unverified; do not publish Scholomance acquisition as fact until checked. Test one warlock order rather than speculative stock.
4. **Satchel of Cenarius — later/niche.** [Recipe lead](https://wowforevertalents.com/professions/tailoring/specialty-bags/#r-27725) describes a 32-slot herb bag using Mooncloth and Black Lotus. [Pattern lead](https://bindonforever.com/item/22312) suggests Cenarion Circle Revered; verify the standing on the actual vendor tooltip, cost and sources. The unlock and inputs make this a lower-priority order-only test.

## Enchanting

1. **Enchanted Leather — strong cross-profession hypothesis.** [Forever spell](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17181/enchanted-leather) lists Annora in Uldaman, Runed Truesilver Rod, one Rugged Leather and one Lesser Eternal Essence for one leather, no listed cooldown. [Forever profession listing](https://wowforever.wclbox.com/en/zhuanye/enchanting) lists skill 250. Specialty bags and other crafts can create demand, but compare output prices against both ingredients before buying stock.
2. **Enchanted Thorium Bar — second conversion.** [Recipe lead](https://wowforevertalents.com/professions/enchanting/smaller-headings/#r-17180) lists one Thorium Bar plus three Dream Dust per bar and a trainer source. Confirm learn skill and rod at trainer. Demand depends on affordable, accessible downstream crafts; do not assume a market because expensive recipes exist.
3. **Shield – Greater Stamina — accessible service candidate.** [Forever formula](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=16217/formula-enchant-shield-greater-stamina) lists Enchanting 265, +9 Stamina, and Horde vendor Daniel Bartlett in Undercity with limited stock. [Recipe lead](https://wowforevertalents.com/professions/enchanting/shield-enchants/#r-20017) lists six Dream Dust and Runed Truesilver Rod. Confirm materials and vendor price before publication. A customer-material service avoids speculative inventory.
4. **Crusader — high-value but costly unlock.** [Formula lead](https://bindonforever.com/item/16252) · [recipe lead](https://wowforevertalents.com/professions/enchanting/weapon-enchants/#r-20034): Enchanting 300, 4 Large Brilliant Shards and 2 Righteous Orbs, Runed Arcanite Rod; formula reportedly drops from Scarlet Archmage / Scarlet Spellbinder. Potential repeat weapon commissions, but verify Forever drop, binding, effect and costs; compare formula cost against expected orders. No guaranteed volume.
5. **Cloak – Greater Fire Resistance — watch, not default.** [Recipe lead](https://wowforevertalents.com/professions/enchanting/cloak-enchants/#r-25081) reports +15 fire resistance, Enchanting 300 and Nexus Crystals; [formula lead](https://bindonforever.com/item/20732) reports a Cenarion Circle gate. Blizzard's Onyxia guidance suggests a possible buyer, but availability of inputs, other options and realm price are unknown. Confirm source, reputation and supply before suggesting it.

## Blacksmithing

1. **Dark Iron Shoulders — targeted fire-resistance watch.** [Forever tooltip](https://wowforever.wclbox.com/api/open/v1/items/11605): bind-on-equip plate, equip level 53, 514 armor, +10 Stamina and +10 Fire Resistance. [Recipe lead](https://wowforevertalents.com/professions/blacksmithing/plate-pauldrons/#r-15295) lists Blacksmithing 255, 6 Dark Iron Bars and one Heart of Fire at a Black Anvil; [plan lead](https://bindonforever.com/item/11615) indicates a BRD drop. Verify plan, forge access and material sources. Potential Onyxia preparation is not proof of demand or a required item; assess travel and bar costs, and favor an order.
2. **Dark Iron Mail — alternate buyer segment.** [Recipe lead](https://wowforevertalents.com/professions/blacksmithing/mail-chestguards/#r-15293) · [item lead](https://bindonforever.com/item/11606): reported Blacksmithing 245, 10 Dark Iron Bars, 2 Hearts of Fire, Black Anvil, mail with +12 Fire Resistance. Verify the tooltip and plan source independently. Compare with Shoulders before adding both; the distinction is mail versus plate buyers.
3. **Thorium Shield Spike — investigate carefully.** [Recipe lead](https://wowforevertalents.com/professions/blacksmithing/miscellaneous/#r-16651) and [item lead](https://bindonforever.com/item/12645) indicate the *user* needs Blacksmithing 250, restricting the buyer pool. Confirm actual use restriction, recipe source and competing shield enhancements before recommending.

Lionheart Helm and other expensive bind-on-equip crafted epics were proposed during research, but are **outside this slice's no-auction-house-epics constraint**. Do not add them to Gold-making without a product-scope decision.
