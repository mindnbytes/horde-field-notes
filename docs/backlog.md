Implemented as `#/paladin/protection/readiness`, adjacent to Gear and Road to 60. It covers **leveling dungeons**, **level-60 dungeons**, and **raid bosses**, with a character-sheet example and separate notes on temporary block.

## Profession gold-making — initial slice implemented

Blacksmithing, Enchanting and Tailoring have compact Gold-making test/watch candidates. Blacksmithing starts with Silver Rod and Heavy Weightstone; expand only where a candidate adds a distinct use or buyer.

- For each candidate show the recipe source and gates, what you sell (finished item, consumable or service), who buys it and why, ingredients, output quantity, and when to skip. Start with examples to verify, such as bags for Tailoring, rods and utility items for Blacksmithing, and useful enchants or materials for Enchanting.
- Distinguish a fast seller from a high-margin but slow seller. Explain how to check price and demand on your realm; don't call a recipe profitable everywhere. Compare net proceeds after applicable fees against the current value of the inputs, **including materials you gathered yourself**. Call out recipe acquisition cost, deposits and relisting where applicable.
- Give one practical pricing example and recommend testing a small batch first. No live Auction House integration or gold guarantees in the first pass.
